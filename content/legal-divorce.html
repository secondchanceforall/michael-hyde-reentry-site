Pro Se Divorce & Custody Action Plan

Legal Filing Approach

Filing will be initiated under Wisconsin’s no-fault divorce statute (Chapter 767), citing irretrievable breakdown of the marriage. Representation will be completely pro se, using Form CV-410A to seek a full waiver of the court filing fee (.50), minimizing out-of-pocket tracking costs to –.

Children & Structured Custody Proposal

To prioritize parental placement stability, the following formal restructuring is proposed:

  • 100% Legal & Physical Custody: Awarded formal designation to the maternal grandfather, who acts as the primary de facto caregiver.
  • Structured Visited Routine: One day per week, four hours per visit.
  • Support Allocation: Redirection of ongoing and arrears payments directly to the grandfather's household.
