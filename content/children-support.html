💰 The $15,000 arrears solution — good faith 'loan' framework

📄 Formal request to maternal grandfather

“I am asking you to formally ‘loan’ me the $15,000 child support arrears that the court currently shows I owe to your ex-wife. In practice, after the court enters a new order naming you as the payee, I will make a single monthly payment to you that includes:

  • (1) my ongoing monthly child support obligation (calculated under Wisconsin child support guidelines, 17% of income for two children);
  • (2) an agreed-upon monthly amount toward the $15,000 arrears until the full balance is repaid.

Every dollar — both ongoing support and arrears repayment — goes directly to you for the benefit of the children in your home. In exchange for you ‘loaning’ me the arrears (i.e., accepting repayment rather than your ex-wife), I fully satisfy the $15,000 debt through those monthly payments.”

Why this works for grandfather:

✔ He gains legal custody and direct child support income.
✔ The $15,000 arrears will be repaid to him, not lost to a non-custodial grandmother.
✔ Provides a predictable, court-monitored financial stream for the children’s needs.
