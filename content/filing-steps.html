Step-by-Step Filing Plan

Pro Se Legal Roadmap & Document Preparation Protocol

Overview

This roadmap details the exact steps required to navigate the self-represented filing process in Kenosha County, ensuring all statutory guidelines are executed systematically and correctly.

Step 1 — Gather Required Documents

Before initiating any paperwork, the following verification materials must be organized and on hand:

  • Proof of Wisconsin residency (Minimum 6 months verification)
  • Proof of Kenosha County residency (Minimum 30 days verification)
  • Proof of income (Current pay stubs for financial disclosures and fee waiver)
  • Historical income proofs (2024 & 2025 completed tax returns)
  • Complete itemized list of all marital assets and liabilities
  • Existing statutory child support order documentation
  • Original certified marriage certificate

Step 2 — Mandatory Court Forms

The following official Wisconsin Court System documents are required for this action and are prepared via the self-help assistant:

  • FA-4104V — Summons with Minor Children
  • FA-4108V — Petition with Minor Children
  • FA-4119V — Admission of Service (or process server alternative)
  • FA-4120V — Declaration of Service
  • FA-4126V — Stipulation for Temporary Order with Minor Children
  • FA-4130V — Motion for New (De Novo) Hearing
  • FA-4136V — Petition for Appointment of Guardian ad Litem
  • FA-4138V — Income and Expense Statement
  • FA-4139V — Financial Disclosure Statement
  • FA-4147V — Proposed Parenting Plan
  • FA-4150V — Marital Settlement Agreement with Minor Children
  • CV-410A — Petition for Waiver of Fees and Costs

Filing Protocol & Procedural Steps

Step 3 — Fee Waiver Submission

File Form CV-410A concurrently with the divorce petition. This step addresses the statutory $184.50 filing fee and the matching $10 child support administrative add-on, attempting to bring standard baseline court processing costs down to $0 based on income qualification thresholds.

Step 4 — E-Filing Execution

Submit all verified documents electronically through the Wisconsin eFiling portal (efiling.wicourts.gov). The filing goes under the supervision of the Clerk of Circuit Court, Rebecca Matoska-Mentink, and is reviewed within the Family Court system overseen by Court Commissioner Elizabeth Pfeuffer.

Step 5 — Formal Service of Process

Formally serve the filed paperwork to the spouse. Prioritize certified mail with an Admission of Service (the lowest-cost method at approximately $10.00). If formal service is rejected, budget an external process server allocation (~$60) to fulfill statutory notice mandates.

Step 6 — Mandatory 120-Day Waiting Period

Abide by the mandatory Wisconsin statutory cooling-off window. This 120-day clock begins immediately upon proof of service and cannot be skipped. Use this timeframe to draft and finalize the comprehensive Marital Settlement Agreement with the maternal grandfather.

Step 7 — Final Hearing Appearance

Represent yourself pro se at the final hearing scheduled after the 120-day window closes. Present the completed Marital Settlement Agreement (Form FA-4150V) containing the formal placement and arrears structured updates to the Court Commissioner for final decree approval.
