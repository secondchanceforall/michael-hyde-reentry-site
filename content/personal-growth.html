🧍‍♂️ Who is Michael Hyde — the reformed man

Michael is not a statistic. He's a son living with his father rent‑free to redirect every dollar toward restitution, a father who owes $12k in child support arrears and refuses to run from it, a worker showing up before sunrise six days a week, and a man who has voluntarily eliminated alcohol, smoking, and destructive environments. He represents the gritty, unglamorous work of real rehabilitation.

After incarceration, Michael could have surrendered to bitterness. Instead, he built a structured, accountability‑driven life plan in collaboration with Associate Professor Albert Harris (UW‑Parkside) and his probation officer. His world now runs on discipline: 5 AM wake‑ups, 12‑hour shifts, financial tracking, and a promise to stay lawful. He is rebuilding trust not with grand promises, but with daily routines and transparent budgets.
