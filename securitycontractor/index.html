Illinois Private Security Contractor

Experience Requirement  ·  3 years within the last 5 years
IDFPR • VE Forms

✅ You must have 3 years of qualifying experience in the 5 years immediately before you apply. Choose ONE of the three routes below.

1 Private Security Company Manager
Have you worked full-time for at least 3 years within the last 5 years as a manager or administrator for an Illinois-licensed private security company?
Form VE-SAC required
2 Proprietary Security Force Manager

You must have managed a proprietary security force with 30+ employees, registered with Illinois IDFPR.

Examples of such firms in Illinois: Archer Daniels Midland (ADM), Caterpillar Inc., State Farm Insurance, Advocate Aurora Health, and other large industrial or healthcare corporations with in-house security.
Have you worked full-time for at least 3 years within the last 5 years as a manager of a proprietary security force with 30 or more employees, registered with Illinois IDFPR?
Form VE-PSF required
3 Security Supervisor

You worked full-time for 3 years as a supervisor in one of these qualifying positions:

  • An in-house security unit for a corporation with 100 or more employees
  • Military police or a related military security unit
  • Federal, state, or local law enforcement
  • Certain qualifying positions with a State's Attorney, Public Defender, or Department of Corrections
Have you worked full-time for at least 3 years within the last 5 years as a security supervisor for a qualifying employer or agency (in-house security, law enforcement, military police/security, or other qualifying government agency)?
Form VE-DSC required

⚠️ Important — Guard Experience Alone May Not Qualify

Your 10+ years as a security guard does not automatically satisfy this requirement. The key question is: Did you have qualifying MANAGERIAL or SUPERVISORY experience during the last 5 years?

If YES, the correct VE form can document that experience. If you were only a security officer/guard and never had qualifying management or supervisory duties, your 10 years of guard experience alone may not satisfy the Private Security Contractor experience requirement.

Illinois IDFPR specifically requires the appropriate VE-SAC, VE-PSF, or VE-DSC form to document qualifying experience.
