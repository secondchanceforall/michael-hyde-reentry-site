RECITALS

WHEREAS, Debtor currently owes child support arrears in the amount of $15,000 according to prior court orders, but said arrears are legally recorded as payable to the maternal grandmother, who does not have custody of the children; and

WHEREAS, the maternal grandfather has physical placement and care of the children 100% of the time; and

WHEREAS, the parties desire to redirect the arrears obligation and establish a repayment plan directly to the maternal grandfather, which the Court will incorporate into a modified child support order as part of the pending divorce action.

1. ARREARS REPAYMENT ACKNOWLEDGMENT. Consistent with the Divorce Decree entered by the Kenosha County Circuit Court (the "Decree"), Creditor acknowledges that the total child support arrears balance as certified at the time of the Decree is $__________. The parties agree that Debtor shall satisfy this arrears obligation in full through installment payments routed through the Wisconsin State Disbursement Unit (SDU) as set forth herein. Creditor shall not seek separate enforcement of any prior arrears order naming the maternal grandmother, that obligation having been resolved by the Decree.

2. REPAYMENT TERMS. Debtor shall pay $417 per month toward the certified arrears balance, for a term of 36 months, beginning on the first day of the first full month following entry of the Decree, or as otherwise directed by the Court. All arrears payments shall be made through the Wisconsin SDU, which shall disburse funds to Creditor in accordance with the Decree. Total arrears to be repaid: $15000.

3. ONGOING CHILD SUPPORT. In addition to the arrears installment payment, Debtor shall pay ongoing monthly child support in the amount of $816, as ordered by the Court and consistent with Wisconsin child support guidelines established at the final hearing. All ongoing support payments shall be made through the Wisconsin SDU, which shall disburse funds to Creditor. Combined monthly obligation routed through SDU: $1233. The ongoing support amount is subject to modification pursuant to Wis. Stat. §767.59 upon petition by either party demonstrating a substantial change in circumstances, including but not limited to a significant change in Debtor's income or the needs of the minor children.

4. CONSISTENCY WITH DECREE. This Agreement is entered into following, and is consistent with, the Decree, which has: (a) resolved all prior child support obligations owed to the maternal grandmother; (b) designated Creditor as the legal payee for all child support obligations of Debtor; (c) awarded Creditor 100% legal custody and primary physical placement of the minor children; and (d) authorized the child support repayment structure set forth herein. This Agreement supplements the Decree and shall be attached thereto as Exhibit A.

5. DEFAULT & REMEDIES. If Debtor fails to make payments as agreed, Creditor may seek enforcement through the court, and any unpaid balance shall become due on motion. However, good faith modifications may be agreed in writing.

6. VOLUNTARY AGREEMENT. Both parties enter this Agreement freely, without coercion, and acknowledge that it is in the best interests of the minor children.
