Pro Se Divorce & Custody Action Plan

Wisconsin Chapter 767 · No‑Fault Pro Se Representation

Filing initiated under Wisconsin’s no-fault divorce statute (Chapter 767), citing irretrievable breakdown of the marriage. Representation will be completely pro se, using Form CV-410A to seek a full waiver of the court filing fee ($0.50 administrative cost), minimizing out‑of‑pocket tracking expenses.

👨‍👧‍👦 Children & Structured Custody Proposal

To prioritize parental placement stability and protect the best interests of the children, the following formal restructuring is proposed:

  • 100% Legal & Physical Custody: Awarded formal designation to the maternal grandfather, who acts as the primary de facto caregiver and provides a stable, nurturing home environment.
  • Structured Visitation Routine: One day per week, four hours per visit, ensuring consistent and meaningful parent-child bonding under supervised yet flexible conditions.
  • Support Allocation: Redirection of ongoing child support and all arrears payments directly to the grandfather's household to cover daily care, education, and health expenses.
📌 Legal strategy alignment: This arrangement reinforces family continuity while Michael Hyde focuses on employment, financial rehabilitation, and full compliance with probation. Redirected support ensures children's needs are met without disruption.

📁 Required Documentation & Forms

All court forms available at wicourts.gov/forms plus Kenosha County Family Court. Key packets:

  • CV-410A – Petition to Waive Filing Fees & Costs
  • FA-4104V – Summons with minor children
  • FA-4119V – Marital Settlement Agreement worksheet
  • FA-4147V – Order for income withholding (child support)

🧾 Financial & Arrears Alignment

Pursuant to the structured reentry budget, child support obligations will be paid directly to the maternal grandfather once custody is transferred. This ensures accountability while reducing arrears ($12,000 outstanding as of current filing). Monthly support obligation of $850 will be redirected, and a supplemental arrears repayment schedule will accelerate the balance.

🔄 Reentry Financial Integration

Michael Hyde's reentry budget (submitted to Probation Officer Noah Gilbert) prioritizes child support stability and arrears reduction. With housing provided rent-free, the monthly budget allocates:

  • Ongoing child support: $850/month (redirected to grandfather)
  • Arrears acceleration: $1,000/month → aiming to eliminate $12k arrears within 12 months
  • Full compliance: automatic income withholding ensures consistent payments.

This divorce & custody plan officially designates the maternal grandfather as payee, aligning with Wisconsin's best-interest standard and providing immediate financial protection for the children while the father follows a 72-hour work week rehabilitation schedule.

✅ Budget surplus ~$1,620/month for additional arrears/debt

📌 Critical Legal Considerations

  • Pro Se filing strategy: all documents self-prepared, using free legal aid from Kenosha Legal Action Center to review before submission.
  • Parental rights & relocation: the maternal grandfather will serve as custodial guardian; the decree will include specific visitation enforcement provisions.
  • Child support redirection: request an Income Withholding Order (FA-4147V) sending all payments to grandfather’s address, superseding prior DCF payee.
  • Waiver of filing fees: CV-410A based on current low income and incarceration history; expected to be granted, making filing cost effectively $0.50.
