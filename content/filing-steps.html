Step-by-Step Filing Plan

Pro Se Legal Roadmap — Kenosha County Circuit Court, Wisconsin

Overview

This roadmap details every step required to file for divorce as a self-represented (pro se) petitioner in Kenosha County. Check off items as you complete them — your progress is tracked above. All court forms open the local PDF copies saved in this site's content/ folder. Fill them out, then print using your browser's Print menu (Ctrl+P / Cmd+P).

Step 1 Gather Required Documents

Organize every item below before touching a single form. Each entry tells you exactly which document satisfies the requirement and where to get it.

  • Wisconsin Residency — minimum 6 months
    Current Wisconsin driver's license or state ID showing a Wisconsin address, plus one corroborating item: utility bill, bank statement, lease agreement, or official mail postmarked within the last 6 months with your name and address.
  • Kenosha County Residency — minimum 30 days
    Any government-issued mail, lease/rental agreement, employer letter, or parole/supervision address-verification letter from your agent confirming your current Kenosha County address dated within the last 30 days.
  • Current Proof of Income
    Two most recent pay stubs. If employed through a work-release or reentry program, obtain a signed income letter on employer letterhead. Required for Fee Waiver (CV-410A) and Income & Expense Statement (FA-4138V).
  • Tax Returns — 2023 & 2024
    Copies of filed federal returns. If you don't have them, get free IRS transcripts instantly at irs.gov/individuals/get-transcript. Feeds into Financial Disclosure Statement (FA-4139V).
  • Complete Itemized List of All Marital Assets & Debts
    Write out: all real estate (address + estimated value + mortgage balance), vehicles (year/make/model/estimated value), bank accounts (institution + last 4 digits + balance), retirement accounts, personal property of value over $500, and all debts (creditor name, account number, balance). This directly populates FA-4139V and FA-4150V.
  • Existing Child Support Order & Payment History
    Locate your current court order showing the case number and ordered amount. Request a full payment history printout from the Wisconsin Support Collections Trust Fund (WI SCTF): call 1-800-991-5530 or visit dcf.wisconsin.gov/cs. Documents current arrears balance for FA-4150V.
  • Original Certified Marriage Certificate
    Required by the court. Order a certified copy at vitalrecords.wi.gov or in person at the Kenosha County Register of Deeds, 1010 56th St, Kenosha (bring photo ID). Fee applies.
  • Certified Birth Certificate(s) for Each Minor Child
    Order from vitalrecords.wi.gov if not on hand. Needed to support the Proposed Parenting Plan (FA-4147V).
  • Spouse's Full Legal Name, Current Address & Date of Birth
    Required for the summons and to complete service of process. If current address is unknown, contact the Kenosha Clerk of Courts before filing — you may need to serve by publication, which has additional steps and costs.

What you need to fill this out:
  • Full legal names of both spouses exactly as they appear on the marriage certificate
  • Kenosha County as the filing county
  • Respondent's last known mailing address (for service)
  • Action type: Divorce with Minor Children
This form formally notifies your spouse that a divorce action has been filed. It is served alongside FA-4108V.
FA-4108V Petition for Divorce with Minor Children
Open & Fill ↗
▼
What you need to fill this out:
  • Date and location of marriage (from certified marriage certificate)
  • Full names, dates of birth, and ages of all minor children
  • Current address and county of residence for both parties
  • Grounds: irretrievable breakdown of the marriage
  • Brief statement of requested child custody and placement arrangement
  • Whether you are requesting child support, maintenance, or property division
This is the primary filing document that opens the case. File it together with FA-4104V and CV-410A.

FA-4139V Financial Disclosure Statement
Open & Fill ↗

What you need to fill this out:
  • All real estate: address, estimated fair-market value, outstanding mortgage balance
  • All vehicles: year, make, model, estimated value, loan balance
  • All bank/credit union accounts: institution name, last 4 digits, current balance
  • Retirement and investment accounts: institution, account type, current value
  • All debts: creditor name, account number, current balance, monthly payment
  • Life insurance: carrier, face value, cash value, named beneficiary

FA-4150V Marital Settlement Agreement with Minor Children
Open & Fill ↗
▼
What you need to fill this out:
  • Completed FA-4147V (Parenting Plan) — attach as exhibit
  • Completed FA-4139V (Financial Disclosure) for both parties
  • Agreed final division of all marital property and debts (who gets what, who owes what)
  • Final child support amount and payment method
  • Arrears repayment structure negotiated with maternal grandfather
  • Both parties' signatures — must be signed before a notary public
This is the primary final order document presented to Commissioner Pfeuffer at the final hearing. Get it notarized before the hearing date.

Steps 3–7 Filing Protocol & Procedural Steps

Step 3 — Fee Waiver Submission

File Form CV-410A at the same time as your divorce petition (FA-4108V). Hand both documents to the Clerk of Circuit Court's window simultaneously. If approved, the standard $184.50 filing fee and the $10 child support administrative surcharge are waived, bringing your baseline court filing cost to $0.

Step 4 — E-Filing Execution

Submit all verified documents electronically through the Wisconsin eFiling portal. Create a free account, select Kenosha County Circuit Court — Family Division, and upload your completed PDFs. Filing is reviewed by the Clerk of Circuit Court, Rebecca Matoska-Mentink, under Family Court Commissioner Elizabeth Pfeuffer.

Wisconsin eFiling Portal ↗ eFiling Help & Guide ↗ Kenosha Clerk of Courts ↗
Step 5 — Formal Service of Process

After filing, serve the summons (FA-4104V) and petition (FA-4108V) on your spouse. Attempt certified mail with an Admission of Service form (FA-4119V) first — lowest cost at approximately $10. If the spouse refuses or does not sign, hire a private process server (budget ~$60) to complete personal service and file the Declaration of Service (FA-4120V) with the court. The 120-day waiting period does not begin until proof of service is filed.

Step 6 — Mandatory 120-Day Waiting Period

Wisconsin law requires a minimum 120-day waiting period beginning from the date proof of service is filed with the court. This window cannot be waived or shortened. Use this time productively: negotiate and finalize the Marital Settlement Agreement (FA-4150V) with the maternal grandfather's participation, complete the Parenting Plan (FA-4147V), and gather the notarized signatures needed before the final hearing.

Step 7 — Final Hearing Appearance

After the 120-day window closes, the court will schedule a final hearing before Commissioner Elizabeth Pfeuffer. Appear pro se and present the completed, notarized Marital Settlement Agreement (FA-4150V) containing the final child placement schedule, child support amount, arrears repayment plan, and property division. If both parties have signed the agreement, the hearing is typically brief and the commissioner issues the final divorce decree that day.
