THIS AGREEMENT is made and entered into as of ___________, by and between:

Debtor/Father: _______________ (“Michael Hyde”), and

RECITALS

WHEREAS, Debtor currently owes child support arrears in the amount of $15,000 according to prior court orders, but said arrears are legally recorded as payable to the maternal grandmother, who does not have custody of the children; and

WHEREAS, the maternal grandfather has physical placement and care of the children 100% of the time; and

WHEREAS, the parties desire to redirect the arrears obligation and establish a repayment plan directly to the maternal grandfather, which the Court will incorporate into a modified child support order as part of the pending divorce action.

1. ARREARS REDIRECTION & LOAN ACKNOWLEDGMENT. Creditor (Grandfather) agrees to accept repayment of the total arrears balance of $15,000 as a “loan” in form only, meaning that Debtor shall pay that sum directly to Creditor in installments. The parties agree that this satisfies the prior arrears obligation in full once paid, and Creditor shall not seek enforcement of the prior order naming the maternal grandmother.

2. REPAYMENT TERMS. Debtor shall pay to Creditor the sum of $417 per month toward the arrears, for a term of 36 months, beginning on the first day of the month after the final divorce hearing or as otherwise ordered by the court. Total arrears repaid: $15000.

3. ONGOING CHILD SUPPORT. In addition to the arrears payment, Debtor shall pay ongoing monthly child support in the amount of $816 directly to Creditor, consistent with Wisconsin child support guidelines. Combined monthly payment to Creditor: $1233.

4. COURT ORDER MODIFICATION. The parties agree to request that the Kenosha County Circuit Court enter an order: (a) vacating any prior child support judgment in favor of maternal grandmother; (b) naming Creditor as the legal payee for all child support; (c) incorporating this repayment plan; and (d) awarding maternal grandfather 100% legal custody and placement.

5. DEFAULT & REMEDIES. If Debtor fails to make payments as agreed, Creditor may seek enforcement through the court, and any unpaid balance shall become due on motion. However, good faith modifications may be agreed in writing.

6. VOLUNTARY AGREEMENT. Both parties enter this Agreement freely, without coercion, and acknowledge that it is in the best interests of the minor children.
